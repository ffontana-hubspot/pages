What we've heard

Your world, as we understand it.

BestStart is one of New Zealand's most established early childhood providers. That scale brings real opportunity — and, we know, a few growing pains. Here's the landscape you've shared with us.

260
Centres

Across New Zealand — a network large enough to deserve a platform built to match it.

1:n

One family, multiple children, potentially several centres — one story to tell.

The friction

What's getting in the way today.

None of this is new to you — but it's worth naming clearly, because every part of the HubSpot case below responds directly to one of these.

01

A fragmented tech stack

APT, OnePlace and Autopilot each do a piece of the puzzle, but none speak to one another. No single view of a family exists across them, so your central team and centre administrators fill the gaps manually — which costs time and introduces risk.

02

Family & child relationships

Most CRMs weren't built for the reality of early childhood: one family, multiple children, each at different centres, across different waitlists — sometimes a parent enquiring at two sites in the same town for the same child. That complexity gets flattened inside generic contact records.

03

Waitlists that feel personal

Some of your centres do a beautiful job calling families every two to four weeks. The challenge is keeping that warmth consistent across all 260 locations — without burning out the teams who do it well, and without leaving families at less-resourced centres feeling forgotten.

04

Nurture that scales gently

With parents enquiring up to 18 months before a start date, Autopilot's basic flows leave many of those long-lead relationships without the gentle guidance they need. Families go quiet, centre managers can't keep everyone warm, and opportunities quietly slip away.

What good looks like

A single journey — from first enquiry to that first day.

Each of these outcomes is achievable, and we've seen organisations of similar complexity get there. They're the measuring sticks we'd want to hold ourselves to, together.

01

One platform, one journey

A single system covering the full enquiry-to-enrolment path — from a parent's first Google search, through tour booking and nurture, to the handshake with APT on the child's welcome morning.

02

Family and child records that mirror reality

Records that reflect how centres actually work: one family unit, linked children, multiple locations, and communication tokens that pull through the right centre name and child's details without re-keying.

03

Centralised waitlists with a personal touch

Manage all 260 waitlists from one dashboard — without losing the warmth that makes BestStart different. Centres still drive the conversations; central just makes sure nobody falls through a crack.

04

Smart, gentle nurture

SMS and email, triggered at the right moment — meeting families where they are, whether they're 18 months out and just starting to research, or a fortnight from their tour and making final decisions.

05

A clean handshake with APT

Two-way integration so nobody gets contacted twice. Enrolment data flows seamlessly; the team stop double-handling; APT stays the system of truth for enrolled families, and HubSpot handles everything upstream.

The solution

One platform. Three moving parts, working as one.

Each piece handles a distinct stage of the enquiry-to-enrolment journey — and because they share the same contact record, there's no double-handling, no data re-entry, and no family falling through the cracks.

Capture & nurture
For Justin, the regional marketing execs, and every centre-level campaign

Ads, landing pages, forms, SMS and email. Smart workflows guide long-lead families from first curiosity to booking a tour — automatically, at scale, across 260 locations from a single template library.

  • Website forms, live chat & Breeze-powered conversational capture
  • Centralised templates with local tokens (centre name, days, pricing)
  • SMS + email nurture flows up to 18 months out
  • Revenue attribution across Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn
  • AEO dashboard for visibility across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity
One family view
For the central enquiry team and every centre administrator

Link multiple children to a single family record. See every touchpoint across every centre a family has engaged with. Trigger the right follow-up at the right moment — without the manual note-keeping that happens today.

  • Family object with linked parent and child records
  • Centre object with capacity, availability, area manager
  • Anonymous visitor tracking for up to 6 months pre-enquiry
  • Mobile app for centre directors on the floor
  • Granular permissions per role (central, area, centre)
Waitlists & support
Replacing OnePlace, without losing what works

Replace OnePlace's repurposed ticketing with a proper queue built around preferred start dates, days needed, and personal conversations — delivered at scale across all 260 centres with SLAs that flag when follow-ups slip.

  • Dynamic waitlists by preferred start date, days and times
  • Centre, area-manager and central dashboards
  • SLA alerts when a tour follow-up hasn't happened on time
  • Post-tour surveys feeding back into the record
  • Two-way APT integration for enrolled families
